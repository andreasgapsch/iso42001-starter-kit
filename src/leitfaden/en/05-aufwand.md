# 5 Effort in person-days

The table gives reference values for the **internal time** up to stage 1 readiness, in person-days (PD) of eight hours. It counts the time of everyone involved: AIMS lead, top management, IT, business units, purchasing, HR, data protection.

**Assumptions.**

- SME with 50 to 500 employees, one to three sites.
- 5 to 10 AI systems in scope, mostly purchased AI (SaaS, AI in standard software), at most one in-house development.
- No high-risk AI under the EU AI Act. The module for the AI Act adds about 2 to 4 PD; a high-risk system adds considerably more. The NIST AI RMF module adds about 3 to 5 PD (chapter 10).
- You work with the templates and the AIMS Manager. Without the kit, expect two to three times the effort for writing.
- Path with ISMS: ISMS certified under ISO/IEC 27001, core processes running.
- AIMS lead with about 40 to 50% of their working time; they carry about 60% of the person-days, top management, IT, business units and, with an ISMS, the ISMS team the rest (chapter 4).
- Extended scope (several in-house developments, AI in the product, high risk, several companies): with an ISMS about 45 to 55 PD.

**How to read the figures.**

- The lower value applies to a small scope, an experienced AIMS lead and quick approvals. The upper value applies to more systems, several sites or little experience with management systems.
- The hours per phase shown in the AIMS Manager are lower. They only measure the time spent in the tool. The person-days here include workshops, clarifications with suppliers, training and approvals.
- The biggest difference between the paths lies in step 8 (document control, suppliers, incidents and training are set up from scratch) and step 9 (audit programme and first internal audit without routine).

**External costs.** In addition:

- **Certification body:** stage 1 and stage 2 together typically take a few auditor days; the body calculates the duration under ISO/IEC 42006 from size, number of AI systems and complexity and states it in the quotation. A combined audit with the ISMS saves days. Annual surveillance audits and recertification in year three come on top.
- **Standard:** ISO/IEC 42001 from SNV, DIN or ISO.
- **Internal audit:** the auditor needs knowledge of ISO/IEC 42001. Even with an ISMS, the ISMS auditors often do not know the standard: plan 1 to 2 days of familiarization or external support, without an ISMS 2 to 3 days. The ISMS team audits only where it does not itself work on the AIMS project.
- **Optional:** legal review of borderline cases under the EU AI Act.
