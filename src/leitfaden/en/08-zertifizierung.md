# 8 Certification

A certificate is voluntary. Many SMEs first build the AIMS for themselves, for customers or for a supplier audit and decide later. If you want to be certified, choose the body early: quotations in week 4 with an ISMS, in weeks 7 to 8 without, choice two weeks later (chapter 4). There are still few accredited bodies for ISO/IEC 42001, and their audit dates are often booked well in advance.

## The right body

- **Accredited for ISO/IEC 42001.** Choose a certification body whose accreditation covers ISO/IEC 42001. Accreditation is granted under ISO/IEC 17021-1; for ISO/IEC 42001 the body also meets the requirements of ISO/IEC 42006. In Switzerland, accreditation is granted by **SAS** (Swiss Accreditation Service), in Germany by DAkkS. Accreditations by national bodies that are members of the International Accreditation Forum (IAF) recognition arrangement are recognized internationally if the scope falls within the arrangement; check this for ISO/IEC 42001 in IAF CertSearch. Check the scope of accreditation in the public register, not only on the body's website.
- **ISO/IEC 42006.** This standard from 2025 sets out what certification bodies for AIMS must meet in addition to ISO/IEC 17021-1: auditor competence for AI, calculation of audit duration, process. Ask in the quotation whether the body works to it.
- **Combined audit.** With an ISMS, a body that audits ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 42001 in one audit pays off. It saves days and appointments.
- **Auditors with sector knowledge.** Ask about experience with AI in use at deployers, not only in software development.

Certificates without accreditation are cheaper but carry little weight with customers and in tenders.

## Process

| Stage | What is checked | What you prepare |
|---|---|---|
| Stage 1 | documentation, scope, readiness for stage 2; often partly remote | document package from D-25, stage 1 report from the AIMS Manager |
| Stage 2 | whether the AIMS works as described: interviews, samples, evidence per control | contact persons, evidence to hand, site visit |
| Certification decision | the body reviews the audit report; major nonconformities must be resolved | action plan for minor nonconformities |
| Surveillance audit years 1 and 2 | samples, effectiveness, handling of changes | annual calendar, internal audit, management review |
| Recertification year 3 | full review for the next three years | as for stage 2 |

## What the auditor wants to see

The evidence list in D-25 is ordered by clause. The key points:

- approved policy, scope and statement of applicability, with dates;
- AI inventory, risk register and impact assessments per system, with approvals;
- records from operation: approvals of new tools, tests, monitoring, training;
- a completed internal audit and a recorded management review;
- a register of nonconformities with causes and effectiveness checks.

Answer questions in the audit with evidence, not with explanations. What you cannot show counts as not done.
