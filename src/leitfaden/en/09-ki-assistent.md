# 9 Faster with the AI assistant

The kit includes an AI assistant as a skill for Claude and for ChatGPT/Codex. It knows the method, the templates, the IDs and the project file of the AIMS Manager. It asks questions, suggests wording, reviews drafts and fills in templates. It is optional: everything in this guide also works without AI.

## The 12 modes

Each mode fits a step. You start a mode by its name or number, for example ‘Mode 3: AI inventory’.

## Working well with the assistant

- **Hand over the project file.** Export the project file from the AIMS Manager and give it to the assistant. Import the results back into the tool. That way the project file remains the single source of truth.
- **Keep the standard at hand.** The assistant contains no text from the standard. Read the clauses it names in your own copy.
- **Check the results.** The assistant delivers drafts. The AIMS lead remains responsible for the content; only the person in charge can approve.
- **Not for legal questions.** Classifications under the EU AI Act are proposals. Borderline cases belong with a lawyer.

## Data protection and confidentiality

Whatever you give the assistant leaves your organization and goes to the provider of the AI service. Therefore:

- Use a business plan (Team, Business, Enterprise) under which inputs are not used for training, and check the storage location and retention period.
- Do not enter personal data, customer data or trade secrets that you would not also show an external consultant. For AIMS work, the functions and names of AI systems are usually enough.
- Record the assistant itself as an AI system in your inventory (D-10). It is subject to your use policy like any other tool.

The AIMS Manager itself works fully offline in the browser. It sends no data to the internet.
