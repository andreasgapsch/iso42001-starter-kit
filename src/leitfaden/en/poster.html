Path with or without an ISMS

With ISMS (ISO/IEC 27001)

  • run jointly: document control, internal audit, management review, corrective action
  • adopt the risk scales, add impacts on people
  • 13 supplementary sheets instead of new documents
  • integration matrix D-23
  • about 25 to 35 PD internally, 90 days to stage 1

Without ISMS

  • build lean core processes alongside: D-26, D-20, D-21, D-22
  • own, simple risk method (D-06)
  • all core templates as separate documents
  • consider external help for the first internal audit
  • about 45 to 65 PD internally, about 150 days to stage 1
